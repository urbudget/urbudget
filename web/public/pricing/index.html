What the free app includes

All of it. There are no tiers and nothing is locked. UrBudget is open source under the AGPL-3.0, and the code is on GitHub. Optional services such as SimpleFIN and your chosen AI provider charge separately.

  • Monthly envelopes with rollover, credit-card payment envelopes and targets
  • Quick assign from what's underfunded, last month or your averages
  • A register you can type into, with transfers, splits, filter chips and bulk edits
  • Twelve reports, from spending by category to net worth, forecast and cash age
  • Scheduled transactions and a loan planner
  • SimpleFIN bank sync for supported US and Canadian banks, with review before saving; SimpleFIN subscription sold separately
  • Bank file import (CSV, OFX, QFX, QIF) with duplicate detection and matching
  • YNAB import from its export file or its API, and export to YNAB's CSV format
  • Several budgets, automatic backups and a full JSON export
  • The budget assistant, using your own ChatGPT plan or API key

Questions

Is the app really free?

Yes. Every feature is free, with no time limit, and the code is open source. Optional device sync is $3 a month. Optional bank sync requires a separate SimpleFIN subscription; the assistant uses your own AI plan or API key. None is required to use the app.

Is there a budgeting app that doesn't need a subscription?

UrBudget is one. The app itself is free and works without any subscription. Optional device sync costs $3 a month to keep several computers in step automatically; without it, you can export a budget on one computer and import it on another.

Actual Budget is also free, and a few other budgeting apps are sold once. YNAB alternatives compares them with the subscription apps, fairly.

Does bank sync need the device sync plan?

No. Bank sync and device sync are independent. SimpleFIN connects supported US and Canadian banks; you pay SimpleFIN directly. Its listed price is $1.50 a month or $15 a year, plus applicable tax. Check SimpleFIN's current pricing and bank availability before subscribing. Follow the setup guide.

Is UrBudget open source?

Yes, under the AGPL-3.0. The code is on GitHub.

Do I need an account?

Not for the app. Sync needs one, so your subscription and encrypted budgets have an owner. You sign in with your email address and a one-time code; there's no password.

What does device sync cost, and what does it do?

$3 a month. It keeps your budgets the same on all your computers. Each computer encrypts a budget with your sync passphrase before uploading it, so the server can't read it.

Do I need Sync to use UrBudget on two computers?

No. You can export a budget on one computer and import it on the other. Sync does that for you, continuously.

What if I forget my sync passphrase?

We can't recover it, because we never see it. Your budgets are still on your computers, so reset sync in UrBudget, choose a new passphrase and they upload again.

What happens if I cancel Sync?

Sync keeps working until the end of the month you've paid for, then stops. The budgets on your computers aren't affected. Cancel from your account page under Manage subscription.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, for Sync. Email support@urbudget.app within 14 days of starting it and we'll refund it. The app itself is free. See the refund policy for the details.

Who handles the payment?

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Polar Software, Inc. (“Polar”), the Merchant of Record for all our orders. Polar handles payment, sales tax and VAT, receipts, and refunds.

Depending on where you live, Polar may add sales tax or VAT at checkout. Your receipt comes from Polar.

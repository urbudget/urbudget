UrBudget privacy policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Who we are. UrBudget, the desktop and mobile apps and the website at urbudget.app, is run by Ahmad Horanieh, an independent developer (“UrBudget”, “we”, “us”). We are the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: support@urbudget.app.

The short version

  • Your budgets live in a database on your own device. Optional Sync sends us encrypted copies we cannot read. Optional AI features send selected information directly to your chosen provider; reporting a reply sends us only the text you review and submit.
  • There are no analytics services, advertising or tracking tools on this website or in the app. We keep a few daily counts ourselves, described under Technical data, and they record nothing about who.
  • We collect what we need to sign you in, deliver purchases, run Sync, review reports and answer your emails.
  • Payments are processed by Polar for website purchases, by Google Play for purchases made through Google Play, and by Apple for purchases made through the App Store. We never receive your full card details.
  • You can delete your account and everything synced to it in the app's Account & sync settings.

Budgets on your device

UrBudget stores each budget in a SQLite database in the app's data folder on your computer or phone, along with local backups and assistant history. Exports go to the location you choose. You don't need an account to budget locally. Budget files reach our servers only if you enable Sync; a report can separately include budget information if you choose to leave it in the submitted text.

Local budget files and assistant history are not separately encrypted by UrBudget. Your device's screen lock, storage encryption and account permissions protect them. Android's operating-system backup and device-transfer features may copy local budgets, settings and assistant history, depending on your device and backup settings. Exports may also be backed up or shared by the destination you choose. Android connection secrets are encrypted using Android Keystore and stored outside Android's automatic backup area.

The app contacts our servers only when it needs to:

  • Sign in and sync, if you turn on Sync (see below).
  • Verify a Google Play or App Store purchase, if you subscribe to Sync or restore it through Google Play or the App Store (see Purchases).
  • Submit an assistant report, only when you review its text and select Send report (see Assistant reports).

Some optional features connect your device directly to other companies, under your own account with them. These requests do not pass through UrBudget's servers:

  • YNAB import: the app uses your YNAB token to download the budget you select. File import reads the file you choose on your device.
  • SimpleFIN bank connections: the app exchanges your setup token for a SimpleFIN access URL and uses that credential to retrieve your connected accounts, balances and transactions directly from SimpleFIN Bridge. SimpleFIN and its bank-connection providers handle bank authorization; UrBudget does not receive your bank login details. Imported account and transaction data becomes part of your local budget and is included in encrypted Sync if enabled.
  • AI assistant: your messages, attached files and budget details the assistant looks up to answer you go to OpenAI or Anthropic, whichever you choose, using your ChatGPT plan or your own API key. This can include financial information. Review attachments and use the assistant only with information you want that provider to process. Notes you ask the assistant to keep are stored in your budget and go to the provider with each message; you can read and remove them in the assistant's settings. Conversation history is stored on your device.
  • Bank texts (Android, optional): with SMS access, the app lists who has texted your phone so you can choose your bank’s senders; that list isn’t kept or sent. It keeps texts from the senders you choose on your phone. They go to your AI provider when Bank messages reads them (when you open it, or as they arrive if you turn that on), and when you ask the assistant about bank texts and it searches them: texts it finds are kept in that conversation’s history on your device until you delete it. Once a text is reviewed, the app keeps on your phone what became of it and the balance it gave, not the text itself. Only the transactions you add become part of your budget and of Sync. Stop and clear in Bank messages removes the texts, review and settings it kept.

Those companies' terms and privacy policies govern what you send them. Disconnecting a connection or clearing local history does not delete information already processed by a provider; use that provider's controls for deletion requests.

What we collect, and why

Your account (only if you create one)

An account is needed for Sync and to associate Google Play and App Store purchases with your UrBudget account. We keep your email address, when the account was created, and the sign-in sessions you have open (for the app, with the device name you choose, if any). We send a one-time sign-in code to your email address instead of using a password; codes expire after a short time.

Purchases

Polar processes your payment as the merchant of record and collects the details it needs for that, including your payment details, name or country where required for tax, and email address. From Polar we receive what we need to deliver your purchase: your email address, what you bought, transaction and subscription identifiers, and their status (for example active, canceled or refunded). We never see or store your full card number.

For purchases made through Google Play, Google processes your payment. The app sends Google an obscured identifier derived from your UrBudget account ID to bind the purchase to that account. The app sends our server the purchase token and product identifier; we verify these with Google and store the token, its account association, product and internal entitlement identifiers, purchase state, expiry and acknowledgment state. We use this information to grant or restore Sync access and respond to renewals, cancellations and refunds. Google handles payment details; we do not receive your full card number.

For purchases made through the App Store, Apple processes your payment. The app gives Apple an identifier derived from your UrBudget account ID (an app account token) to bind the purchase to that account. The app sends our server the transaction Apple signed, and Apple sends our server notifications about it, such as renewals, billing problems, expiry and refunds. The signed transaction includes its identifiers, the product, purchase and expiry dates, the App Store country and price, and the app account token. We store the transaction identifiers, the app account token, the product, the purchase, expiry and refund dates, the auto-renewal and billing-retry status, and whether it was a test purchase. We use this information to grant or restore Sync access and respond to renewals, cancellations and refunds. Apple handles payment details and your Apple Account; we do not receive your full card number.

Sync (only if you subscribe)

Each device encrypts your budgets with a key made from your sync passphrase before uploading them. We store:

  • the encrypted budget files and their encrypted names, which we cannot read;
  • each file's size, a revision number, the time it was uploaded and the device name that uploaded it;
  • a random salt and an encrypted check value that lets your devices test a passphrase. We never receive your passphrase or the key made from it, so we cannot decrypt your budgets or recover them if the passphrase is lost.

Email

We send only the email you need: sign-in codes and messages about your account or subscription. No newsletters or marketing unless you ask for them. If you write to us, we keep the conversation so we can help you.

Android test

To test the Android app before it's on Google Play, you join UrBudget's testers group on Google Groups, which is the test's list of testers, and then become a tester on Google Play with the same Google account. Google runs both and processes that under its own privacy policy. As the group's owner, we can see its members' email addresses. We use them only to run the test, which Google requires before a new app can be published, and they aren't linked to an UrBudget account.

Before the group, this site took email addresses for the test. If you left one, we keep it and when you gave it, use it only to tell you about the test and, later, that the app is on Google Play, and don't link it to an UrBudget account. To limit abuse, we stored a daily hash of the request's IP address rather than the raw IP.

Assistant reports

Reporting an assistant reply is optional and does not require signing in. Before sending, you can edit or remove private information from the reply and add an optional reason. The form also suggests a line of details to send with the reason (the app and its version, the AI service and model, the tools the reply used and the app's language), which you can edit or remove. We store only the submitted reply (up to 4,000 characters), the reason (up to 1,000 characters), a random report reference and the time received. The app does not attach your account, the rest of the conversation, files or budget database. Text you leave in the form may still identify you or include financial information. Authorized administrators review these reports to investigate inappropriate or harmful output and improve the service; reports are not published.

Our host processes ordinary connection information for the request. For abuse limits, we store a daily hash of the request's IP address rather than the raw IP in the report or its rate-limit entry. Keep the reference shown after sending if you want us to find or delete a report.

Technical data

Like any website, our hosting provider processes your IP address and basic request information (such as the page requested, browser type and time) to deliver the site, keep it secure and limit abuse, for example by rate-limiting sign-in attempts. We don't use this to build profiles or follow you around the web.

We keep five kinds of daily counts, as totals that record nothing about who:

  • Page views: how many times each page of this site was viewed, and where visitors came from (the website that linked here, or the short tag a link carries, like ?ref=hn). The site's own script sends this when a page opens; it sets no cookie and sends nothing that identifies you.
  • Downloads: how many times each installer was downloaded, and which page of this site or which other website the download link was on, or a link's tag. We also note the download totals GitHub shows for the files on UrBudget's releases, and the acquisition and install totals Microsoft's Partner Center shows for UrBudget in the Microsoft Store.
  • Desktop update checks: desktop copies installed outside the Microsoft Store ask urbudget.app for updates after starting and once a day while open. We count those requests per day, version and system. They carry the app's version and your system and processor type, nothing else. Store-distributed desktop copies and the Android app use their store's update mechanism.
  • Desktop copies in use: once a day, a desktop copy of UrBudget tells urbudget.app that it was opened, and whether that's its first time ever or its first today, this week or this month, with the app's version, your system (such as Windows or macOS) and whether it was installed from the Microsoft Store. The first time a copy is used each week, it also sends the date of the week it was first opened, which it shares with every copy first opened that week, so we can see how many people come back. The app keeps the dates it last did this on your device; it sends no identifier, and we keep only the daily totals. You can turn this off in the app's Settings, under Privacy & data, with Count this copy as in use.
  • Account activity: how many sign-ins, sign-in emails, checkouts started, Sync uploads and account deletions happen each day, and of which kind (for example, website or app sign-in).

Visits and downloads by crawlers, scripts and automated browsers are counted separately, so the totals reflect people.

Cookies

This website sets one cookie, and only when you sign in: ub_session, an HttpOnly session cookie that keeps you signed in to your account page. It is strictly necessary for that, and it's removed when you sign out. There are no analytics or advertising cookies.

This website loads no payment scripts. Checkout happens on Polar's own site, where Polar may use cookies or similar technologies needed to process the payment and prevent fraud; see Polar's privacy policy.

Our legal bases

Where data protection laws such as the GDPR apply, we process personal data to perform our contract with you (sign-in, Sync, support), to meet legal obligations (such as tax and accounting records), and for our legitimate interests in keeping the service secure, preventing abuse and knowing how many copies of the app are in use.

Who processes data for us

We use a small number of service providers, each only for the job described:

  • Cloudflare hosts the website and the API, including the account database and the encrypted Sync storage. Cloudflare's privacy policy
  • Polar (Polar Software, Inc.) is our merchant of record for payments: checkout, payment processing, tax, receipts, subscriptions and refunds. For payment data, Polar acts as an independent controller. Polar's privacy policy
  • Google Play processes purchases made through Google Play and supplies purchase status so we can provide the entitlement. Google independently processes payment and Play account information. Google's privacy policy
  • Apple processes purchases made through the App Store and supplies signed purchase information and notifications so we can provide the entitlement. Apple independently processes payment and Apple Account information. Apple's privacy policy
  • Resend delivers our emails (such as sign-in codes), so it processes your email address and the message. Resend's privacy policy

We don't sell your personal data, and we don't share it for advertising. We may disclose information if the law requires it, and we would tell you unless legally prohibited.

International transfers

Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, these transfers rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Account and Sync data: for as long as you keep your account. When you delete your account, we delete it along with its sessions and synced budgets.
  • Purchase records: kept to validate purchase status and prevent the same purchase being claimed by another account, and for as long as tax and accounting laws require. Polar, Google and Apple keep their own payment records.
  • Sign-in codes expire within minutes.
  • Support emails: for as long as needed to help you, and then deleted.
  • Android test list: until the Android app is on Google Play, or earlier if you ask us to remove your address.
  • Assistant reports: deleted by daily cleanup once older than 90 days, or earlier when no longer needed or on a deletion request identifying the report. Abuse-limit entries are routinely cleared after their short rate-limit window.
  • Records of problems, such as an email we couldn't deliver or sign-in attempts that hit a limit, with the email address or IP address involved: 90 days.

Your choices and rights

You can delete your account in the app's Settings, under Account & sync, using Delete account. You can also email support@urbudget.app to ask for a copy of your data, a correction or deletion, or to object to or restrict processing. To request deletion of an assistant report, include its reference because reports are not linked to your account. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act, and the right to complain to your data protection authority. We'll answer within one month. To stop the desktop app's daily count of copies in use, turn off Count this copy as in use in its Settings, under Privacy & data.

Deleting your account removes its sign-in sessions and synced budgets. It does not remove local files, exports or operating-system backups from your devices, or a report that has no account association. Purchase records may remain for the reasons above. Before deleting an account, we ask the payment provider to stop renewals of its known subscriptions; if this fails, deletion does not proceed. You can also manage or cancel a Google Play subscription in Google Play. We can't cancel an App Store subscription: only you can, in your Apple Account settings, and it keeps renewing until you do, so the app reminds you before you delete your account. You can delete local budgets in the app and manage backups through your operating system.

Security

Everything between your device and our servers travels over HTTPS. Synced budgets are end-to-end encrypted (XChaCha20-Poly1305, with a key derived from your passphrase using Argon2id). Sign-in uses one-time codes, and the website's session cookie can't be read by scripts. We keep as little data as we can, because data we don't have can't leak.

Children

UrBudget isn't directed at children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant and you have an account, we'll also tell you by email before it takes effect.

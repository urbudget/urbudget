Sync

Sync is on. In UrBudget, open Settings → Account & sync.

Budgets are encrypted on your computers before they're uploaded, so we can't read them. Canceling doesn't affect the budgets on your computers.

This deletes your account and the encrypted copies of any budgets synced to it. The budgets on your computers aren't touched.

Features

Budget

Start with what's in your accounts and give all of it a category: rent, groceries, the car insurance that's due in March. Spending comes out of its category. If you overspend somewhere, move money over from another one, and whatever is left carries into next month.

  • Targets for weekly, monthly and yearly bills, or for an amount by a date.
  • Credit card spending moves money into the card's payment category, so the bill is covered when it comes.
  • Quick assign fills underfunded categories or repeats last month, and shows the changes before applying them.

Transactions

Click a row in the register and type. Payees remember their last category, and amount fields do arithmetic, so 42.10+8 works.

  • Transfers and split transactions are edited in the row.
  • Search with filters such as payee:, amount:>100 or is:cleared.
  • Select many rows to categorize, approve or clear them together.

Scheduled transactions and loans

Enter your paycheck, rent and subscriptions once. UrBudget adds them when they're due and leaves them unapproved until you've looked at them, and upcoming bills count toward what their category needs.

The loan planner shows the amortization schedule, what an extra payment saves, and the payment it takes to finish by a given date.

Twelve reports

From spending by category to net worth and a forecast of your balances. Each one filters by date, account and category, and exports to CSV. Try five of them below, with a sample household's numbers.

Try asking

  • Your real numbers

    Before it answers, it reads this month, your transactions, how you've categorized each payee, reports, the forecast and your loans. Its figures come from your budget.

  • You apply every change

    Moving money, auto-assign, categorizing, new transactions, renaming and merging payees: each is a card with Apply and Discard, and an applied card can be undone.

  • Receipts and statements

    Receipts and statements as photos or PDFs, and CSV or Excel files. Statements go to the import review, with the totals checked and duplicates matched.

  • Reports and spreadsheets

    A month in review as a PDF, or transactions, budgets and spending as an Excel workbook, built from your budget so every row is exact.

Your AI account

Sign in with ChatGPT and your Plus or Pro plan covers it, or use your own OpenAI or Anthropic API key, kept in an encrypted vault protected by your operating system. UrBudget adds no AI fees.

What it sends

Your messages, the files you attach and the figures it looks up go to OpenAI or Anthropic, whichever you choose. From a spreadsheet, only the rows it reads. Conversations stay in your budget's folder.

What it won't do

Delete anything, change settings or move real money, and it isn't a financial adviser. It uses generative AI, so check what it proposes; every reply has a Report link.

Claude Desktop, too

Claude Desktop and other apps that support MCP can read the open budget: months, transactions, reports and forecasts. They can't change anything.

Bring your YNAB budget over.

Paste a YNAB token, click Import, and your whole budget comes over: every account, category, transaction and month, with your targets, notes and scheduled transactions.

  • Checked to the cent. UrBudget recalculates every month and compares it with YNAB's own numbers: account balances, each category's assigned, activity and available, and Ready to Assign. Any difference is listed.
  • Try it side by side. Import again whenever you like and only what changed in YNAB comes down. What you changed in UrBudget is kept, and if both changed, you choose.
  • Read-only. UrBudget never changes anything in YNAB. The token is used for the import, or saved in the encrypted vault if you choose Remember.

Read the switching guide or compare UrBudget and YNAB.

To make a token, open YNAB's Account Settings, then Developer Settings, and choose New Token. No token? YNAB's export file works too, without targets, notes or scheduled transactions. UrBudget isn't affiliated with YNAB.

Connect your bank. Or import its files.

Use optional SimpleFIN bank sync for supported US and Canadian banks, with a separate subscription paid to SimpleFIN. Press Sync to fetch transactions and review them before saving.

Or download your transactions from your bank as a CSV, OFX, QFX or QIF file. You see each row before it's added, duplicates are skipped, and rows that match transactions you already entered are paired with them. A statement PDF, or a photo of one, can go through the assistant into the same review.

Your budget lives on your computer.

Each budget is a SQLite file in the app's data folder. UrBudget works offline, needs no account, and keeps automatic backups. Optional device sync uploads an encrypted copy of your budget. Bank sync fetches transactions through SimpleFIN, and the assistant sends the information you choose to your AI provider. See the privacy policy for how each optional service handles data.

Sealed with your passphrase

Device sync, if you want it. For $3 a month, sync keeps your budgets the same on all your computers. Each computer encrypts a budget with your passphrase before uploading it (Argon2id and XChaCha20-Poly1305), so the server only holds data it can't read. If you forget the passphrase, your budgets are still on your computers: reset sync and upload them again. Turn on sync

No tracking on this website. There are no ads, no third-party analytics and no tracking cookies. Page views are counted per day, without cookies or anything that identifies you; the privacy policy has the details.

Pay nothing.

YNAB is $109 a year and Monarch Money $99.99, so a $100-a-year subscription comes to $500 over five years. UrBudget is free.

Questions

Something else? Write to support@urbudget.app.

Is UrBudget really free?

Yes. Every feature is free, with no time limit, and the code is open source. The only paid part is optional device sync, at $3 a month.

Is UrBudget open source?

Yes, under the AGPL-3.0. The code is on GitHub.

Do I need sync to use two computers?

No. You can export a budget on one computer and import it on the other. Sync does that for you, continuously.

Does it connect to my bank?

Yes, optionally, through SimpleFIN for supported US and Canadian banks. SimpleFIN charges separately from UrBudget and device sync. Press Sync to fetch transactions, then review them before saving. You can also import bank files or enter transactions yourself. See availability, cost and setup.

Which systems does it run on?

Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) and macOS 12 or later. There's no phone or web version.

How do I get updates?

The Microsoft Store version updates itself through the Store. The version from this site looks for updates when it opens and once a day. When there's one, an Update button appears at the top of the window, and Install & restart saves your budget and opens the new version. Nothing is installed until you choose it.

Where is my data stored?

Each budget is its own SQLite file in UrBudget's data folder on your computer, with its backups next to it. The download page lists the folders for Windows, macOS and the Microsoft Store version.

Can I get a refund?

For sync, yes, within 14 days of starting it; the app itself is free. Email support@urbudget.app, or see the refund policy.

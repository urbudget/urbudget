      Who came back

      Copies first opened the same week (Monday to Sunday, UTC), and the share of them used again in each week after. Bots don't come back, so this is the surest sign of people. The current week isn't over yet.

      Update checks by version

      Copies from 1.0.2 on that were installed outside the Store ask urbudget.app for updates a few seconds after they start, and once a day while open, so each check is about one launch. Nothing identifies a computer, so these aren't users or installs, but they include copies older than the usage counts.

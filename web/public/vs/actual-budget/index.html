UrBudget vs Actual Budget

Both are free, open-source envelope budgets that keep your data on your side. Actual's desktop app works on its own, and syncing between devices means running or renting a server. UrBudget is a native app with optional encrypted sync and nothing to host.

Side by side

UrBudget prices cover the app and any device sync shown. Optional bank sync requires a separate SimpleFIN subscription; see cost and availability.

Which one fits you

Actual Budget, if you

  • use Linux, or want to budget in a browser
  • want a choice of bank providers beyond SimpleFIN, including Enable Banking in Europe or other regions Actual supports
  • are happy to run a server for sync, or pay a host to
  • still have a YNAB4 budget to bring over

UrBudget, if you

  • want a native Windows or Mac app with nothing to host or keep up to date on a server
  • want sync between your computers that's encrypted and just works, for $3 a month
  • are leaving YNAB and want a one-click import, checked month by month, that you can repeat while you decide
  • want a loan planner, twelve reports and an optional budget assistant that uses your own ChatGPT plan or API key

Actual is a good project, and both are free and open source, so the choice comes down to how you want sync to work. If you're happy to run a server, or want Linux or a browser, pick Actual. UrBudget is for people who'd rather not think about servers.

How UrBudget compares

UrBudget is free, open-source envelope budgeting, kept on your own computer. Here's how it compares with the apps people usually weigh it against.

  • YNAB alternatives Twelve budget apps compared on price, platforms, method, bank sync and where your data lives.
  • Mint alternatives What happened to Mint, and eleven replacements compared on price, bank sync and budgets.
  • UrBudget vs YNAB The same method, free instead of $109 a year, and a budget file you own.
  • UrBudget vs Actual Budget Both free and open source: a server to run if you want sync, or a native app with encrypted sync built in.
  • UrBudget vs Buckets Two desktop envelope budgets: $64 once with Linux and shared budgets, or free.
  • UrBudget vs Principal Plan Two offline envelope budgets for Windows and Mac: $39.99 with a view-only phone app, or free with sync between computers.
  • UrBudget vs Goodbudget Envelopes on the web and phones, free or $80 a year, or a free desktop app that keeps your budget in a file.
  • UrBudget vs EveryDollar Ramsey's zero-based app, free or $79.99 a year, or a free app with bank file imports and balances that roll over.
  • UrBudget vs Monarch Money A $99.99-a-year tracker connected to your banks, or a free envelope budget kept on your computer.
  • UrBudget vs Copilot Money A $95-a-year tracker for Apple devices and US banks, or a free envelope budget for Windows and Mac.
  • UrBudget vs Moneydance A $65 Quicken-style finance manager with investments, or a free envelope budget.
  • UrBudget vs Quicken A yearly subscription with investments and bank downloads, or a free envelope budget.
  • How much does Quicken cost? Every Quicken plan's first-year and renewal price, what still works if you stop paying, and the total over five years.
  • UrBudget vs Microsoft Money For Money Plus Sunset users: a current envelope budget for Windows and Mac, free. What it replaces, and what it doesn't.

UrBudget vs YNAB

Both use the same envelope method: give the money you have a job, spend from those jobs, and move money when plans change. They differ in where your budget lives and how you pay for it.

What it costs over time

Over five years, YNAB's yearly plan comes to $545. UrBudget is free, or $180 if you also keep sync on the whole time. YNAB's prices over 1 to 10 years has the full calculation, and every YNAB price change since 2015.

Which one fits you

YNAB, if you

  • want transactions to arrive from your bank on their own (where YNAB supports your bank)
  • budget on your phone as you spend
  • share one budget with a partner or family

UrBudget, if you

  • budget at your computer and want the budget in a file you own
  • would rather not give a budgeting app your bank login
  • want a free app instead of $109 a year
  • are leaving YNAB and want your history to come with you, checked month by month

Switching takes one click

Paste a YNAB token into UrBudget and choose Import & save. Your accounts, transactions, categories, targets, notes and scheduled transactions come over, and every month is checked against YNAB's own totals. You can keep using YNAB while you try it, and import again to pull in what changed. See how switching works.

Questions

Is there a free YNAB alternative?

Yes. UrBudget is free and open source; device sync, SimpleFIN bank sync and your chosen AI service are optional and charged separately. Actual Budget is free too, and Buckets and Principal Plan are sold once. YNAB alternatives compares them with the subscription apps.

Does UrBudget work offline?

Yes, completely: the budget is a file on your computer, and nothing needs a connection except sync and the optional assistant. The difference from YNAB isn't offline use so much as where the budget lives: YNAB keeps it in your YNAB account, and UrBudget keeps it in a file you own.

Can I try UrBudget without leaving YNAB?

Yes. The import reads from YNAB and never changes anything there. Keep using YNAB, and import again whenever you like to pull in what changed.

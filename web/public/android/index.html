UrBudget for Android

Use the Google account that's on your Android phone.

    Open the group
  2. 2

  3. 3

    Install it from Google Play

    Tap Become a tester, then Download it on Google Play.

    Get it on Google Play

Once it's installed

  • Keep it for 14 days. Google lets a new app go public only after 12 testers have stayed in its test for 14 days in a row. Keep it installed and open it now and then.
  • Tell us what's wrong. If something is broken or confusing, write to support@urbudget.app.

What's in the Android app

  • The whole budget. Envelopes and targets, transactions, scheduled bills, the twelve reports and the loan planner.
  • Your bank's texts, ready to add. If your bank texts you when money moves, the AI assistant you connect turns those texts into transactions for you to review. It's optional, and asks before reading any texts.
  • The same budget as your computer. Device sync keeps them in step, encrypted with your passphrase. Without it, a backup from the desktop app opens on the phone.
  • English and Arabic, with full right-to-left support.

Questions

Google Play says the app isn't available

Google Play shows the test only to Google accounts in the testers group. Check that you joined the group with the account that's signed in to Google Play, wait a few minutes, then open the Google Play link again.

Does it cost anything?

No. UrBudget is free on Android, as it is on Windows, Mac, Linux, iPhone and iPad. The only paid part is optional device sync, at $3 a month.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 8.0 or later, on phones and tablets, foldables included.

Will the group send me email?

No. It's only how Google Play knows you're a tester. Only UrBudget can post to it, and doesn't, and other testers can't see who's in it.

Can I leave the test?

Yes. Open the Google Play link, choose Leave the program, then uninstall the app. You can leave the group on Google Groups too.

Budget on your computer meanwhile

UrBudget for Windows, Mac and Linux is free, and its budgets come to the phone with you.

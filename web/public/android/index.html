Android, in testing

UrBudget for Android

Your envelope budget, on your phone. Before a new app can go on Google Play, Google has people test it for 14 days. Leave the email address of the Google account on your Android phone, and we'll send you the link to install the test version from Google Play. It's free, like the rest of UrBudget.

Use the address you sign in to Google Play with. Google only lets that account in.

We use the address only to invite you to the test and to tell you when UrBudget is on Google Play, and then delete it. To be taken off sooner, write to support@urbudget.app. See the privacy policy.

How joining works

  1. Leave your Google account's email above. Use the one you sign in to Google Play with on your phone.
  2. We add you to the test. Your address goes on UrBudget's list of testers on Google Play. Nothing is sent to you yet.
  3. We email you a link. Once Google has approved the test, open the link on your phone, signed in to that Google account, and tap Become a tester.
  4. Install UrBudget from Google Play. It updates through Google Play like any other app.

What's in the Android app

  • The whole budget. Envelopes and targets, transactions, scheduled bills, the twelve reports and the loan planner.
  • Your bank's texts, ready to add. If your bank texts you when money moves, the AI assistant you connect turns those texts into transactions for you to review. It's optional, and asks before reading any texts.
  • The same budget as your computer. Device sync keeps them in step, encrypted with your passphrase. Without it, a backup from the desktop app opens on the phone.
  • English and Arabic, with full right-to-left support.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. UrBudget is free on Android, as it is on Windows and Mac. The only paid part is optional device sync, at $3 a month.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 8.0 or later, on phones and tablets, foldables included.

Can I leave the test?

Yes. Open the same link and leave the program, or uninstall the app. To have your address taken off our list, write to support@urbudget.app.

Budget on your computer meanwhile

UrBudget for Windows and Mac is free, and its budgets come to the phone with you.
